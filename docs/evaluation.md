Generated by `python -m ws.evaluate` on the synthetic retailer (seed 9): 50 stores, 5,000 workers, two years of half-hourly demand.

## 1. Demand forecast

Rolling origin, the last 12 weeks, every store: each week forecast from everything before it. WAPE = total absolute error / total demand.

| | Half-hour level | Daily totals |
|---|---|---|
| Seasonal profile + promotion and rain effects (`seasonal-drivers-1.0.0`) | 8.1% | 4.4% |
| Baseline: same weekday last week | 14.0% | 10.3% |

The model is better in 50 of 50 stores. Half-hour error is dominated by Poisson noise in the counts, which no forecast removes; the daily figure shows the part that can be learned.
